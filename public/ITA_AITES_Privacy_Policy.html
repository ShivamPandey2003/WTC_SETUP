ITA-AITES Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 18 November 2025

Organisation: International Tunnelling and Underground Space Association (ITA-AITES)

Address: Chemin de Balexert 9, 1219 Châtelaine, Switzerland

Contact: secretariat@ita-aites.org

1. Introduction

ITA-AITES is committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information in compliance with the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and, where applicable, the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. What Data We Collect

We may collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Identification details (name, title, organisation)
  • Contact information (email address, phone number, postal address)
  • Professional details (role, affiliation, expertise)
  • Event-related data (registration details, participation preferences)
  • Technical data (IP address, browser type, cookies for website functionality)

3. Purpose of Processing

Your data is processed for:

  • Managing membership and communications
  • Organising events, conferences, and awards
  • Distributing publications and newsletters
  • Responding to enquiries and providing support
  • Ensuring compliance with legal and regulatory obligations

4. Legal Basis

Processing is based on:

  • Your consent (e.g., newsletter subscription)
  • Contractual necessity (e.g., event registration)
  • Legitimate interests (e.g., improving services)
  • Compliance with legal obligations

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. However, we may share it with:

  • Service providers supporting ITA-AITES operations
  • Event partners, including the host organisation for the ITA World Tunnel Congress and ITA Tunnelling Awards. These hosts are selected from ITA's 81 national member organisations and may be located outside Switzerland, the EU, or Europe.
  • Authorities when legally required

When data is transferred outside Switzerland or the EU, we ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as contractual agreements and compliance with applicable data protection standards.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy or to comply with legal, contractual, and regulatory requirements.

  • Event-related data (such as registration details, participation records, and contributions) may be retained beyond the conclusion of the event for legitimate purposes, including:
    • Maintaining historical archives of ITA-AITES activities
    • Preparing future events and invitations
    • Statistical and reporting obligations
  • Membership and communication data is retained for the duration of your membership and for a reasonable period thereafter to manage renewals or resolve queries.
  • Legal obligations may require us to keep certain records for a longer period (e.g., accounting or tax requirements).

We will keep such data for a reasonable period, typically up to 5 years, unless legal obligations require longer retention. After this period, data will be securely deleted or anonymised in compliance with Swiss FADP.

7. Your Rights

Under FADP and GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us at secretariat@ita-aites.org.

8. Security Measures

We implement technical and organisational measures to protect your data against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss.

9. Cookies and Analytics

Our website uses cookies for functionality and analytics. You can manage cookie preferences through your browser settings.

10. Contact & Complaints

For questions or complaints, contact secretariat@ita-aites.org.

11. Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect legal changes or improvements in our practices. The latest version will always be available on our website.
